Overview

Turning a single number into a clear, trustworthy picture of how couriers earn.

Couriers put in the hours, but the app told them almost nothing about how their pay actually added up. This redesign rebuilds courier earnings from the ground up — replacing a single opaque total with a detailed, transparent breakdown of exactly how, when, and why couriers earned what they did.

Problem

Couriers could see what they earned — but never why.

The existing experience showed couriers a single earnings total and little else. There was no breakdown of what made up that number — no visibility into transit pay versus bonuses versus tips, no sense of how a shift compared to previous ones, and no way to understand which parts of their work were actually driving their income.

That lack of transparency made it hard for couriers to trust the number on their screen, and even harder for them to make informed decisions about when and how to work.

Couriers were earning — they just couldn't see how.

Daily & Weekly Breakdown

Earnings broken down by day and week, with a graph for every part of the story.

The redesign introduces daily and weekly earnings views, each backed by graphs that call out the specific components of a courier's pay — transit pay, bonuses, and tips — instead of collapsing everything into one number. Couriers can now see at a glance where their income is actually coming from, and how it shifts day to day and week to week.

Shift & Order Detail

A full shift summary, down to the order and the route.

Alongside the earnings breakdown, couriers get a detailed shift summary surfacing the metadata that explains their day of work: total tips, total bonuses, online time, active time, orders delivered, and distance travelled.

Beyond the shift-level summary, couriers can drill into order-level detail for a run — including a map of the real route they travelled — giving them a complete, honest record of exactly what a shift involved.

Comparative Insights

Every number, next to how it's trending.

Rather than showing figures in isolation, each metric is paired with a comparison against the courier's previous earning cycle — a small up or down arrow alongside a percentage change (e.g. ↑ 4%). That context turns a static report into something couriers can actually use: a way to see whether a change in strategy, hours, or route is paying off.

Outcome

An earnings experience built on transparency, not just a total.

Couriers now have a complete, trustworthy view of their pay — broken down by component, tracked over time, contextualized against their own history, and backed by the shift and order-level detail to explain exactly how each number was earned. This project is ongoing as the experience continues to roll out.
